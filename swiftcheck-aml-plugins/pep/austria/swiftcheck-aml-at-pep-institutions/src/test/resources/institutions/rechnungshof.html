The President

The Austrian Court of Audit is headed by President Margit Kraker.

As President of the Austrian Court of Audit she is also the Secretary General of the International Organization of Supreme Audit Institutions (INTOSAI).


Since 1 July 2016 President of the Austrian Court of Audit and Secretary General of the International Organization of Supreme Audit Institutions (INTOSAI)
2013-2016 Director of the Court of Audit of the Province of Styria
2014-2016 Vice member of the Management Committee of the European Organisation of Regional External Public Finance Audit Institutions (EURORAI)
2010-2013 Member of the Steering Group on Administrative Reform
2007-2013 Vice director of the Styrian Government’s Office
2003-2013 Supervisory board member of the Styrian Tourism Association
2000-2013 Head of the Styrian Governor’s Office / First vice director of the Styrian Government’s Office
1999-2000 Director of the Styrian Parliamentary Group of the Austrian People’s Party, Styrian Parliament
1998-1999 Member of the Styrian Government’s Constitutional Service
1996-1998 Entry into the Civil Service of the Province of Styria, Director of the Styrian Parliamentary Group of the Austrian People’s Party, Styrian Parliament
1985-1996 Legal expert of the Parliamentary Group of the Austrian People’s Party
1983-1985 University assistant at the Institute of Public Law, Political Science and Public Administration at the University of Graz 
1983-1984 Court training, Higher Regional Court of Graz
1979-1983 Law studies at the University of Graz and graduation with PhD degree
  1960 born in Zeltweg, Styria, Austria
