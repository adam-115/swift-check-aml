Authority of the President of the Supreme Court is set in the law “On Judicial Power”, as well as in other laws – Law, Judicial Disciplinary Liability Law, and Office of the Prosecutor Law, as well as the Advocacy Law, Notary’s Office Law, the Law on Bailiffs and Investigatory Operations Law.
