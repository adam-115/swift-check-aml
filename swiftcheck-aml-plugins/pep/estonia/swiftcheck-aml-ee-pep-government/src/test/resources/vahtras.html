Minister of Economy and Industry of the Republic of Estonia since 16 September 2026.

Liina Vahtras was born on 18 January 1979 in Kärdla. She has four children. Vahtras is a member of the Estonian Reform Party.
 

Career

  • 2025-2026, she was a Member of the Management Board of the Estonian Business and Innovation Agency.
  • 2023-2026, she served as Head of e-Residency at the Estonian Business and Innovation Agency.
  • 2022-2023, she worked as Chief of Staff at the technology company Tuum (Modular Technologies).
  • 2017-2022, she worked as Chief of Staff at Pipedrive.
  • 2016-2017, she was Adviser to the Vice-President of the Riigikogu.
  • 2015-2016, she served as Adviser to the Prime Minister at the Government Office.
  • 2012-2015, she was Head of Marketing and Communications at Nortal.
  • 2010-2012, she worked as Communications Coordinator for the Estonian Reform Party.
  • 2007-2010, she served as Adviser to the Prime Minister at the Government Office.
  • 2006-2007, she was Head of Communications for the Estonian Reform Party.
  • 2005-2006, she was Managing Director of Ciaonet.
  • 1999-2006, she worked as E-Channels Marketing Manager at EMT.
education
Liina Vahtras graduated from Tallinn University of Technology in 2018 with a Master’s degree in Engineering, having completed the E-Governance Technologies and Services programme.
In 2006, she graduated from the University of Tartu with a Bachelor’s degree in English Language and Literature.
 
other

Liina Vahtras’s native language is Estonian. She is proficient in English and has basic knowledge of Russian and French.
 

About the job of the Minister of Economy and Industry

The Minister of Economy and Industry is the head of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications and is responsible for its areas of government, with the exception of drafting the Gender Equality and Equal Opportunities Act.

The minister is appointed and released from office by the President of the Republic on the proposal of the prime minister. The minister will assume office by taking an oath of office before the Riigikogu.

  • In addition, the Minister of Economy and Industry is responsible for foreign trade and the management of the relevant departments of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

  • The Minister of Economy and Industry is a member of the Supervisory Board of the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund.
  • The Minister represents Estonia in the Foreign Affairs Council of the European Union in matters relating to foreign trade, in the Competitiveness Council in matters relating to the internal market, industry, tourism, innovation, and space, and in the Employment, Social Policy, Health and Consumer Affairs Council in matters relating to employment, working environment, employment relationships, equal treatment, and gender equality.
  • The Minister of Economic Affairs and Industry is responsible for the Consumer Protection and Technical Regulatory Authority, the Labour Inspectorate, the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund, the Land Board, the non-profit association Estonian Centre for Standardisation and Accreditation, the Estonian Business and Innovation Agency, the Tallinn Science Park Foundation TEHNOPOL, the Tartu Science Park Foundation, the Estonian Stockpiling Agency (AS Eesti Varude Keskus), KredEx Credit Insurance Ltd (Aktsiaselts KredEx Krediidikindlustus), and Metrosert Ltd (Aktsiaselts Metrosert).

Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communication

The objective of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications is to increase the competitiveness of Estonian companies and thus the prosperity of people.

Organisation of work of the Government of the Republic

The government implements the state’s domestic and foreign policy. The government makes decisions on a collegial basis on behalf of the executive. The competence of ministers in managing ministries and the areas of responsibility of ministers are determined with an order of the prime minister.
