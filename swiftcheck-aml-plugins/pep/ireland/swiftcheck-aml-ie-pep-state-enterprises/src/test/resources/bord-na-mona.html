Leading with purpose to progress into a brighter future

Our Board of Directors oversees the strategy, management and future direction of BnM.

A wealth of experience

Our Board of Directors has twelve members, who are responsible for overseeing and directing BnM and ensuring its long-term success. The Board meets approximately 10 times per year to carry out their duties, which includes appointing the Managing Director and the Company Secretary, and reviewing and approving strategies, plans, budgets, financial statements and performance reports.

One shared vision

Our board members bring decades of experience from a variety of sectors including finance, legal, non-profit, technology, government, agriculture and more. They come together at BnM for one shared vision: to see a climate-neutral Ireland by 2050.
